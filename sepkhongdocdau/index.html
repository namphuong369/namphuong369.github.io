Sep Khong Doc Dau Tools

Personal command-line tools that publish finished short videos to their operator's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page, Sếp Không Đọc Đâu (@sepkhongdocdau), and answer messages sent to that Page.

What it does

The tools are small scripts run from a terminal on the operator's computer. The operator drops finished video files into a local folder. The tools read each file and the notes kept alongside it, write the title, description, caption and tags, pick the next free publishing slot, and publish the video as a scheduled video on the operator's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API v3) and as a scheduled Reel on the operator's own Facebook Page (Facebook Graph API). A separate script checks the Page's inbox, drafts a short reply with an AI model, and sends it as the Page.

Who it is for

One person: the operator who installs it and connects their own channel and Page. It is not a hosted service, it has no user accounts, and it does not act on behalf of anyone other than the Google account and Facebook Page that authorised it.

What it asks permission for

On Google:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos and set their thumbnails on the channel of the account that signed in.
  • youtube.readonly: to read back the channel's name, its list of uploads and their public statistics, so the operator can confirm the right channel is connected and avoid uploading the same video twice.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to manage the operator's own uploads after they are created: change the scheduled publishing time, add videos to the channel's playlists, post the channel's own first comment, reply to comments, and update the channel description and keywords when the operator asks for it.

On Facebook (Meta app Sep Khong Doc Dau Tools, one Page only):

  • pages_manage_posts, publish_video: to publish and schedule Reels, videos, photos and text posts on the operator's own Page.
  • pages_read_engagement, pages_read_user_content, pages_show_list: to read the Page's own posts, their status and the scheduled posts, so nothing is published twice or at the same time.
  • pages_manage_engagement: to post comments as the Page under its own posts.
  • pages_messaging, pages_manage_metadata: to read the Page's inbox and reply as the Page.

It does not request, and cannot use, any other Google or Facebook data.

Where it runs

Entirely on the operator's own computer. There is no server, no database and no third-party service in the path, except that the text of incoming Page messages is sent to Anthropic's Claude to draft a reply. The only other network connections it makes are to Google's and Meta's own API endpoints.
